Options

Files

No files yet. Each converted file appears here with a Save button, counts of pages, tables and images, and notes about anything that did not convert cleanly.
  • PDF: paragraphs, fonts, tables, images and columns are rebuilt from the page layout.
  • PPTX: every slide becomes one page with an editable title, text, tables and pictures.
  • Scanned pages have no text layer and are kept as pictures. No OCR is applied.

Converts well

  • PDF text with fonts, sizes, colours, bold and italic
  • PDF tables, images, multi-column text, page size and margins
  • PPTX titles, bullets, fonts and colours from the theme, filled boxes, tables with merged cells, pictures
  • Password-protected PDFs (you will be asked for the password)

Converts poorly

  • Scanned PDFs stay pictures; no OCR
  • PDF hyperlinks, footnotes and form fields become plain text
  • PPTX charts, SmartArt and embedded objects become a placeholder line; EMF/WMF/SVG and linked pictures are skipped
  • PPTX backgrounds, layout artwork, animations and transitions are dropped; side-by-side shapes become columns
